## Approval record

For each asset approved for distribution, record:

- Reviewer name and role.
- Review date.
- Product distribution model (commercial or non-commercial, source availability, and
  share-alike handling).
- Exact source URL and immutable revision or archive checksum.
- Exact licence text and attribution displayed in the product.
- Compatibility decision and any obligations imposed on derivatives.
